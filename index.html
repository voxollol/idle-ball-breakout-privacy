2. Information processed and why

Information stored on your device

The game uses local storage, including Unity PlayerPrefs, for game progression, completed and unlocked levels, earned Sparks and Shards, upgrades, equipped abilities, unlocked or selected ball skins, daily-quest progress where enabled, audio and vibration preferences, FPS limit, game-speed settings, and purchase entitlements.

This information is used to run the game, remember settings, apply purchases, and maintain progression. An online account is not required for ordinary gameplay. Local information may be lost if the game or its data is deleted, unless a separate platform backup restores it.

Advertising, privacy, and technical information

Where advertising is enabled and permitted, advertising and consent services may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, advertising or device identifiers (subject to device settings and applicable consent), app/device details, ad requests and impressions, ad interactions, consent preferences, and diagnostic or anti-fraud information. The exact fields depend on the SDK, platform, region, and privacy choices.

We use this information to display and measure ads, protect ad services against fraud, fulfil privacy preferences, and operate the advertising system. We do not require access to precise GPS location, camera, microphone, or contacts for the game's core functionality.

Information you send us

If you contact us for support or a privacy request, we may process your email address, the information in your message, and relevant support history so that we can respond and meet legal obligations.

3. Age-related handling and children's privacy

Idle Ball Breakout is intended for a broad audience, including younger players. The game includes an age-gate screen. The entered date of birth is used on the device to determine an age category; the full date of birth is not intentionally saved by the game.

In the currently designed advertising flow, third-party advertising is disabled for users classified as under 18 and for users whose age is unknown. Users can still access the game without viewing ads. We do not knowingly use behavioural advertising directed at children. Before releasing new features or changing age-related advertising, we will review relevant child-privacy and app-store requirements.

If you believe a child's personal information was processed incorrectly, please contact us.

4. Advertising and consent management

When enabled, the game may display a banner ad near the bottom of the screen and offer an optional rewarded video after a level. A rewarded video is not mandatory to continue playing. After the advertising provider confirms the qualifying reward event, the game may grant an additional in-game reward.

The game uses, or is being configured to use, Unity LevelPlay to mediate ads, including Unity Ads and the ironSource ad network where enabled, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and communicate applicable privacy preferences. Google AdMob is used to configure the UMP message; it may also act as an advertising partner if enabled in the mediation setup.

Where required, a consent form appears before advertising services make requests. You may be offered choices to accept, refuse, or configure eligible processing activities, and to reopen privacy options later. Declining optional advertising consent does not prevent access to ordinary gameplay. Depending on your location and consent choices, the game may not serve ads at all.

Buying the Ultimate Pack is designed to remove third-party advertising from the game. We do not treat choosing an age category or buying a product as consent to advertising data processing.

5. In-app purchases

Optional products include premium ball skins, Sparks and Shards currency packs, and the Ultimate Pack (which includes game features and virtual currency). Payments are processed by Google Play or, if the iOS version is released, Apple App Store, rather than through a payment form hosted by us.

The store and in-app purchase SDK may process transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status, store account-related information and verification data to complete purchases, prevent fraud, and restore eligible non-consumable purchases. We do not directly receive the full payment card number from a purchase through the app stores.

Store records and any billing disputes, refunds or account data are also governed by the relevant store provider's policies. In-game currencies have no cash value and are not transferable outside the game.

6. Service providers and links

Depending on the released app's features, country, and privacy settings, information may be processed by the following providers:

  • Unity Technologies / Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads: ad mediation, advertising delivery, performance measurement and technical diagnostics. Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Google UMP; Google AdMob if activated): collection and transmission of consent choices and, where used, advertising-related processing. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: Android purchase processing, installation and platform services. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: iOS distribution, purchasing and platform services if the iOS version is released. Apple Privacy Policy.

Other advertising networks may be enabled through LevelPlay. Before release, we will review the actual activated networks and update this notice where needed. Third parties handle certain information under their own privacy notices and applicable contracts.

7. Retention, deletion, and security

Local game settings and progress normally remain on your device until you clear the application's data, uninstall it, or replace the saved data. We do not currently provide a dedicated game-account deletion service because the game does not require an in-game account.

When a support request is received, we retain correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond or meet applicable obligations. Advertising networks and app stores apply their own retention periods; use their linked privacy policies for details.

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the data and services involved. Data processed by external SDKs may be transmitted over networks using security measures maintained by those providers. No digital system can be guaranteed completely secure.
